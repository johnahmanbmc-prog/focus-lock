Waiting changes

Changes that make the blocker looser wait 24 hours before they happen. You can cancel them any time before then.

    Blocking hours

    YouTube, social media, streaming and your own sites are blocked during these hours. Adult and pirate sites are blocked all the time.

    A window that ends before it starts runs past midnight. Set the same start and end time to block the whole day. Adding hours takes effect right away. Removing or shortening hours waits 24 hours.

          Early unlock

          An emergency way out. Request it and strict mode ends 72 hours later. Your hours and lists stay as they are, and you can re-arm strict mode with one click afterwards.

          Browsers

          Chrome and Brave read their settings from this app every minute. If the app is closed, they keep the last settings they got.

          Set up the extension

          1. In Chrome open chrome://extensions, or in Brave open brave://extensions.
          2. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
          3. Click Load unpacked and pick the extension folder.
          4. Come back here. The browser shows as connected within a minute.

          Once connected, the extension's own settings page becomes read-only and says it is managed by this app.
