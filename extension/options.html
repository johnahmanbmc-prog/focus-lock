Blocking hours

Strict mode

While strict mode is on, you can't change the blocking hours or turn the blocker off until the end time. You can still extend it.

Early unlock

An emergency way out. Ask for an early unlock and strict mode ends 72 hours later. Your hours and lists stay as they are. You can cancel the request at any time.
